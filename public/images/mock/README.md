# Lesson player captures

`lesson-light.png` and `lesson-dark.png` are unaltered 1440 × 900 Playwright
screenshots of https://app.rsquaredacademy.com/ggplot2/bars-fill, captured on
14 November 2026. They show the same real lesson in the app's two themes.

The course's R solution was entered in the editor and submitted to the live R
runtime. The app returned its own Correct feedback and chart. The first-visit
tour and difficulty prompt were dismissed with their UI controls; the editor
divider was dragged upward to show the output. Reduced motion was enabled.
No interface, result, progress figure or chart was drawn or substituted.

These assets are used only by the noindex `/mock-b/` page.
